📚 User Guide — Flashcard AI

Welcome to Flashcard AI, a multi-platform smart study tool. This guide covers the powerful gestures, shortcuts, and AI features built directly into our Web application.

☁️ Getting Started & Sync

  • Google Drive Sync: Sign in with Google to automatically backup your decks and settings to a secure, hidden AppData folder in your Drive. This syncs your decks to Desktop and Mobile seamlessly.
  • Cloud Progress: Your card mastery and quiz session progress are safely saved to our database via your Google account, letting you pick up exactly where you left off on another device.
  • Local Upload: Don't want to use the cloud? Use the "Import JSON" button to load local decks.json backups manually.

⚙️ Deck Management

  • Header & Navigation: The top header automatically collapses when scrolling down to give you more reading room.
  • Sorting & Pinning: Click the pin icon to keep important decks at the top. Use the Sort button to toggle between Newest, Oldest, A-Z, or Z-A ordering.
  • Duplicate Finder: Click the Gear (⚙️) icon on a deck to enter the Deck Manager. Go to the "Find Duplicates" tab to scan your entire deck. The system uses an advanced fuzzy-matching algorithm to detect overlapping questions and lets you clean them up with one click.

🤝 Share & Clone Decks

Easily share your decks with friends while keeping your original data safe.

  • How to Share: Open any deck, click the Share button, and enter the email addresses of the people you want to invite. They will be granted secure access immediately.
  • How to Import: Click the Import button when clicking Add Deck, then paste the Deck ID you received.
  • Clone Mechanism: When someone imports your shared deck, they receive a completely independent clone. Any progress they make or edits they perform will not affect your original deck!

🃏 Flashcard Learning Mode

Active recall mode specifically tailored for blazing fast learning.

  • Flipping: Tap the card, click it with your mouse, or press Space / Up / Down to see the answer.
  • Swiping & Feedback:
    • ✅ Known: Swipe the card to the Right (or use Right Arrow). This moves the card to your Known pile.
    • ❌ Unknown: Swipe the card to the Left (or use Left Arrow).
  • Mouse Drag (Desktop): You can click and drag the card left or right. A color glow will indicate your choice. Release to confirm!
  • Undo: Pressed the wrong button? Hit R or click the 🔄 Undo button to bring the last card back.

📝 Quiz Practice Mode

A resilient multiple-choice testing engine that saves your progress automatically.

  • Question Types: Supports both Single-Choice and Multi-Select (checkboxes) depending on how the AI extracted the exam.
  • Submitting Answers: Click the options to select them. Single-choice questions auto-submit. For Multi-select, choose your options and click "Submit".
    Keyboard: Use Tab to focus an option, and Space to toggle/submit.
  • Session Resilience: If you close the tab halfway through a 100-question quiz, your answers are remembered. When you return, the quiz resumes instantly.

    • Navigation & Gestures

  • Arrows: Click the ← / → buttons or use your keyboard's Left and Right arrow keys to browse back and forth without losing answers.
  • Touch Swipe: Swipe left or right on the screen. A floating purple circle will appear to guide your page turns.
  • Reverse Swipe (Jump to Unanswered): Want to quickly find the first question you haven't answered yet?
    Swipe in one direction, hold, and pull back quickly in the opposite direction.
    The side indicator will turn Blue and release to teleport you instantly. Alternatively, press the \ key.

🤖 AI Scan Tool

Convert your lecture slides and past exams into digital flashcards.

🔑 How to get a FREE Gemini API Key

  1. Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click the "Create API key" button.
  3. Select a project (or create a new one if prompted) and generate the key.
  4. Copy the generated key (it usually starts with AIza...).
  5. Paste the key into the Config & API Keys section in the AI Scan tab on our app and click Add.
  • API Key Setup: Go to the AI Scan tab and input your free Google Gemini API key. Keys are saved securely to your private Google Drive (they never touch our servers).
  • Parallel Extraction: You can add multiple API keys at once. When you submit a batch of images, the system acts as a Worker Pool—rotating through your keys to process multiple PDFs in parallel, heavily speeding up extraction.
  • Auto-recovery: If Gemini returns a malformed extraction, our backend uses advanced Binary Splitting and Regex recovery to salvage the questions without failing the entire batch.
