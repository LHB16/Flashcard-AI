User Guide — Flashcard AI

Getting Started

Flashcard AI is designed for high-efficiency learning with cloud synchronization. To begin:

  • Google Drive Sync: We highly recommend signing in with Google. This creates a secure, private FlashcardAI folder in your Drive to store your decks and progress. This allows you to study across multiple devices (Web, Mobile, Desktop) without losing data.
  • Local Mode: If you prefer not to use the cloud, you can use the "Local Upload" feature to load a decks.json file from your device. Note that progress in local mode is saved only in your browser's memory.

Flashcard Learning Mode

The Flashcard mode uses an active recall system to help you memorize terms quickly.

  • Interaction: Click on the card to flip it and see the definition. You can also use the Spacebar.
  • Feedback: After flipping, indicate your confidence:
    • Swipe Right (or press →) if you recalled the answer correctly.
    • Swipe Left (or press ←) if you don't.
  • Shortcuts:
    • R: Undo your last swipe action.
    • Home: Jump back to the first card in the deck.

Quiz Practice Mode

Quiz mode tests your knowledge with multiple-choice questions and tracks your mastery.

  • Navigation: Use the "Next" button to move through questions. Your answers are saved automatically.
  • Review: Click the Back (←) icon to see previous questions. You can see your chosen answer vs the correct one, but you cannot change answers once submitted.
  • Skip & Defer: Click the Forward (→) icon to skip a difficult question. Skipped questions will reappear at the end of the quiz session.
  • Progress: The circular progress bar at the top shows your current completion percentage.

AI Scan Tool

The AI Scan tool uses Google's Gemini Vision models to turn your images/notes into structured decks instantly.

  • API Keys: You must provide at least one Gemini API Key from Google AI Studio. These keys are saved to your private Google Drive AppData folder and never shared.
  • Parallel Loading: You can add multiple API keys. The system will rotate through them to bypass rate limits, processing dozens of images simultaneously.
  • Image Processing: Select a folder or multiple images. Gemini will extract text, identify questions, and format them into Term/Definition pairs.
  • Syncing: Once the scan is complete, the generated deck is saved directly to your Google Drive and becomes available in your "My Decks" tab.

Management & Tips

  • Pinning: Use the Pin icon on any deck to keep it permanently at the top of your list.
  • Sorting: Toggle between Alphabetical (A-Z) and Reverse Alphabetical (Z-A) sorting to organize large collections.
  • Duplicates: Use the "Manager" (Gear icon) on a deck to find and delete duplicate questions automatically using fuzzy matching logic.
  • Accessibility: The app supports full Light and Dark modes. The setting is remembered across sessions.
