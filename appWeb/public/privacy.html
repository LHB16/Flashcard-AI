Privacy Policy

Welcome to Flashcard AI. We believe your study materials and learning progress belong exclusively to you. This policy outlines exactly what data we process and how we store it.

1. Information We Collect & Where It Is Stored

Flashcard AI operates on a "cloud-sync, private-storage" model. We strictly limit the data saved on our servers.

  • Google Account Info: We collect your Google Email Address and Google ID strictly for authentication and session management.
  • Flashcard Content (Your Decks): Your actual flashcards, questions, and answers are stored exclusively in a hidden AppData folder on your own private Google Drive (decks.json). We do not possess, read, or host your flashcard content on our proprietary databases.
  • API Keys: If you use the AI Scan tool, your Google Gemini API keys are also saved directly in your Google Drive AppData folder (config.json). They are temporarily proxied by our backend during a scan but are never stored on our servers or in our database.
  • Learning Progress: To provide seamless cross-device synchronization (resuming a quiz or study session perfectly on another device), we store your learning progress metadata in our Supabase database. This includes:
    • Which card IDs (UUIDs) you marked as known/unknown in Flashcard Mode.
    • The status of active Quiz Sessions (selected answers, score, question order).

2. How We Handle Image Scanning (Google Gemini)

When you use the "AI Scan" feature to generate decks from images or PDFs:

  • Your files are temporarily sent through our backend servers securely to proxy the request to the Google Gemini AI API.
  • These files are held in RAM (Memory) only for the duration of the scan. Once Google returns the extracted flashcards, the images/PDFs are immediately and permanently discarded from our server memory. None of your scan inputs are logged or stored.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To provide Flashcard AI, we rely on the following secure infrastructure providers:

  • Google Identity & Drive API: For secure OAuth login and storing your data privately within your personal Google Drive account.
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): For extracting text and reasoning over your study materials during an "AI Scan". (Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy regarding API usage).
  • Supabase: Our database provider used to independently store your anonymous learning progress identifiers (Card IDs and numerical scores).

4. Data Sharing

We commit NOT to share, sell, distribute, or rent any of your personal data or study materials to third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain ultimate control over your data:

  • Because your flashcard decks are stored in your Google Drive, you can delete them at any time directly from the application interface, erasing the files completely.
  • You can independently revoke Flashcard AI's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Security Dashboard. Doing so immediately cuts off our ability to read or write to your private AppData folder.
