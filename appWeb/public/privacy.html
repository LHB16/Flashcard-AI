Privacy Policy

Welcome to Flashcard AI. Your privacy is our top priority.

1. Information We Collect

Flashcard AI only collects the minimum information necessary to operate the service:

  • Google Email Address: Used for account identification and session management.
  • Google Drive Access (AppData): To view and manage files created by the application itself within the hidden AppData folder.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Synchronizing your flashcard decks across your devices.
  • Storing your learning progress (card status) to ensure a seamless educational experience.

3. Storage and Security

Flashcard AI uses high security standards to protect your data:

  • All flashcard content is stored directly on your own Google Drive (AppData folder). We do not store card content on our own servers.
  • Learning progress is synchronized via a secure database system (Supabase) based on your Google ID.

4. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We commit NOT to share, sell, or rent any of your personal data to third parties for any purpose.

5. Your Rights

You have the full right to delete your data by removing the application and clearing the AppData folder on Google Drive, or by requesting account deletion through the application settings.
